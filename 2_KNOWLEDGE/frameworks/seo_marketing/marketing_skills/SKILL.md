# Marketing Skills

## Overview
This skill contains mental models and prompt patterns derived from Corey Haines's Marketing Skills repository. Use this when the user asks for CRO (Conversion Rate Optimization), copywriting, or product positioning.

## Mental Models
1. **The 'So What?' Framework**: For every feature, ask "So what?" until you reach the core emotional benefit for the user.
2. **PAS Formula**: Problem -> Agitation -> Solution. Use this for landing page copy.
3. **Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD)**: Customers don't buy products; they hire them to do a job. Frame marketing around the job, not the product category.

## Application
When generating SEO Content Gaps or AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) recommendations, inject these marketing mental models to ensure the suggested content isn't just SEO-optimized, but also conversion-optimized.
