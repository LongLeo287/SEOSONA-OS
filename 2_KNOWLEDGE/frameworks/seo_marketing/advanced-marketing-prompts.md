## 4. Last 30 Days Skill (Trend Jacking)
- **Concept:** SEO content goes stale. Evergreen articles lose CTR over time.
- **Execution:** Implement an agent routine that:
  1. Identifies all "Evergreen" content in the site
  2. Cross-references with Google News/Trends (last 30 days)
  3. Injects fresh, real-time facts into introductions
  4. Result: Drastically boosted CTR and freshness signals
