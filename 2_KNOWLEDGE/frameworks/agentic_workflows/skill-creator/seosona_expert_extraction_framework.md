# Expert Extraction Framework (SEOSONA Method)

This framework is used when capturing knowledge from human experts (e.g., SEO Managers, Content Leaders) to build new SOPs, Workflows, or Agent Skills. It ensures that "edge cases" and "standard procedures" (văn mẫu) are effectively digitized.

## The 5-Question Extraction Model

Whenever a human expert successfully resolves a complex case or establishes a new best practice, ask the following 5 questions to extract their "brainpower" into a system skill:

1. **Input (Đầu vào là gì?):** What was the starting condition, dataset, or problem statement?
2. **Output (Đầu ra là gì?):** What was the final deliverable or expected result?
3. **Process (Cách xử lý là gì?):** What exact steps, tools, or logical leaps were taken to get from Input to Output? (Especially for edge cases).
4. **Evaluation (Đánh giá như thế nào?):** What criteria define "Success" (10/10) versus "Failure"? *Why* are these the criteria?
5. **Conclusion (Đúc kết):** Summarize this into a repeatable heuristic, formula, or "văn mẫu" (standard template) that an AI Agent or junior employee can blindly follow next time.
